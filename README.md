
RegretCalculator.net | The Invisible Math of Opportunity Cost


RegretCalculator.net is a financial engineering tool designed to quantify the "unseen" cost of consumer habits. By calculating the opportunity cost of micro-subscriptions and impulsive spending over a 10-30 year horizon, the tool helps users visualize the long-term impact of current financial decisions through the lens of compound interest.

🚀 The Core Thesis
Most consumers struggle to conceptualize $20/month. They see it as a rounding error. This tool applies a standard 7-10% market return (inflation-adjusted) to reveal the "Invisible Math": that $20 subscription isn't just $240/year; it’s **$15,000+** of lost wealth over a career.

🛠️ Technical Architecture
The application is built with a focus on low-latency calculation and minimalist UX.

Backend: Python / Flask

Frontend: HTML5, Tailwind CSS (Mobile-First Design)

Logic: Custom compounding algorithms handling variable inflation and dividend reinvestment simulations.

Deployment: [https://vercel.com/szs-projects-86d0bf48/regret-calculator-dev/3UyVuvm26JHrzVjDrBXvoKLruUNj]

📈 Key Features
Compound Interest Simulation: Real-time visualization of wealth erosion.

Variable Yield Analysis: Users can toggle between conservative (5%), market average (8%), and aggressive (12%) returns.

Inflation Adjustment: Real-value calculations to provide a more "honest" look at future purchasing power.
